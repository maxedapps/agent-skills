What changed and why

Before: the catalog had a change-report completion artifact with static hotspot cards and abbreviated paths, no generic HTML-report skill, and implement-plan finished after creating the report without independently reviewing the candidate.

After: create-report is skill 17 for explicit standalone HTML reports; change-report critical points expand to failure, protection, complete paths, and one canonical excerpt; implement-plan requires a fresh read-only candidate review before Complete.

Unchanged on purpose: code-review and use-subagents policy; no shared template infrastructure; no historical-report retrofit; no PDF/chart-library/theme-pack work.

The big picture

User-facing reports split by owner. Change reports stay the completion gate; generic reports are opt-in; implement-plan now challenges the exact HTML it is about to hand off.

How a report now leaves the building

  1. Freeze. Scope comes from git status plus staged/unstaged diffs, including untracked files. This handoff file is excluded from counts.
  2. Author. Copy the matching template. Critical points or generic modules are filled from verified evidence only.
  3. Creator QA. Light, dark, popover or dialog, expanded disclosure, and console/network checks.
  4. Candidate review. A fresh reader uses references/artifact-review.md on the exact bytes. Outcome stays out of the HTML.
